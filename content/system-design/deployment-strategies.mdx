## Strategies

### Blue-Green Deployment
Two identical environments. Switch the router.

### Canary Deployment
Roll out to a small subset of users first.

### Rolling Deployment
Update servers one by one.

## Rollbacks
The ability to quickly revert a bad deploy is more important than the ability to deploy quickly.
